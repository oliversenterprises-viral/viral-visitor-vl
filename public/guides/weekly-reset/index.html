When the ViralRefer week resets

Direct answer for search and AI

The contest board counts unique friends from Monday 00:00 UTC until the next Monday. Last week’s friends do not carry over. There is no cash prize.

What resets

  • This week’s friend count on the live board
  • Who is in range for the 7-day banner this week

What does not reset

  • Your referral link — keep sending the same /r/ URL
  • The rule that a visit or a copy is not a credit

Send this week. A friend who tapped last Monday does not move you this Monday. Ladder: Site Drops.

FAQ

Is it my local Monday?

No. It is UTC. Convert once and pin it. The homepage clock is the source of truth.

Can I bank friends for next week?

No. The board is this week only. That is the race.
