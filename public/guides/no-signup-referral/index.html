ViralRefer is a worldwide referral race on one live leaderboard. You do not create an account. You do not give an email to start.

How to get your link

  1. Open viralrefer.app.
  2. Tap Get my referral link.
  3. Send that link in any app (WhatsApp, Texts, Telegram, email, X, or Share…).

You climb only when a friend opens your link and taps Get my link too. Copying the link by itself does not count.

What you can win

There is no cash prize. Two verified friends can add a text line on the homepage this week. This week’s top racer (not the site owner) with three verified friends can claim a 7-day homepage banner for their website. Open worldwide, 18+.
