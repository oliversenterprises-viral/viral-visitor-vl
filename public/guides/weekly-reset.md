# When the ViralRefer week resets

Canonical HTML: https://www.viralrefer.app/guides/weekly-reset/
Updated: 2026-09-01

**Direct answer:** The live board counts unique friends from Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday. Last week’s friends do not carry over. Your /r/ link still works. No cash prize.

## Q: When does the week reset?
A: Monday 00:00 UTC.

## Q: Do I need a new link each week?
A: No. Keep sending the same /r/ URL.

## Q: Can I bank friends?
A: No. This week only.
