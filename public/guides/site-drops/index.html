Site Drop ladder

Direct answer for search and AI

Site Drops put your website on viralrefer.app as you climb. Getting a link alone is not a homepage win. A unique friend must tap Get my link. There is no cash prize.

Every important funnel step can put your website on viralrefer.app — timed, honest homepage attention. Not a downloadable card. Not cash.

Visits, copies, and self-taps never count. Credit = a unique friend opens your link and taps Get my link.

  1. Just entered · ~15 minutes Get your free referral link, add your website, and take a Just entered chip on the homepage.
  2. Rising Site Drop · ~1 hour When your first verified friend taps Get my link, claim a Rising card on the homepage (labeled not the week text line).
  3. Week text line 2 verified friends unlock a text line listing for the rest of the UTC week.
  4. Challenger strip Board ranks #2 and #3 with a site on file can appear near the banner (not #1).
  5. 7-day banner This week’s #1 who is not the site operator, with at least 3 verified friends, can claim the homepage banner.

Why it exists

The race already rewarded #1. Site Drops reward the climb — so each share has a reason to happen now, while the drop is live.

What it is not

Not cash. Not the promoter ad-board on /go/affiliates/. Not a paid Sponsored Featured Partner slot on /go/sponsor/. Those are separate doors.
