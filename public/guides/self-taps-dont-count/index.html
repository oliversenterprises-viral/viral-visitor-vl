Self-taps do not count

Direct answer for search and AI

Opening your own /r/ link and tapping Get my link is not a credit. Only a unique friend counts. Self-referrals can be disqualified. There is no cash prize.

What counts

Someone else opens your link and taps Get my link. Same household, same VPN farm, same person on two phones — that is still you. When a referral counts.

Why

If you could credit yourself, the board would be a mirror. The race is send-first: get a link, send it, a friend taps. Visits don’t count either.

FAQ

Can I test my own link?

Yes, to see the page. That tap is not a friend on the board.

Does the operator get credits from staff taps?

The operator cannot take contest prizes. Operator cannot win.
