When a referral actually counts

Direct answer for search and AI

A credit is not a click. Someone must open your unique link and tap Get my link. Visits, copies, and self-taps are zero. There is no cash prize.

Site Drop ladder: add your site after Get my link → 15-min Just entered. 1 friend → Rising hour on the homepage. 2 → week text line. #1 (not owner) → 7-day banner. Full guide · See it live

People lose races because they think a click is a credit. On ViralRefer it is not.

What counts

Someone opens your unique link (the /r/VIRAL-… URL) and taps Get my link on that page. Then you get one verified friend.

What does not count

  • They only visit and leave
  • You copy the link but never send it
  • You open your own link
  • Bots, farms, or fake unique people

Optional: after you have a link, turn on Ping me in this browser so you hear when a friend actually joins.
