ViralRefer vs referral software vs giveaways

Karanga Oliver · Lawton, Oklahoma · 2026-09-01. No cash prize on ViralRefer.

People search “Viral Loops alternative” or “Gleam alternative” and land on three different jobs. Mixing them wastes a week.

1. Public race (ViralRefer)

The board lives on viralrefer.app. You get a /r/ link in about 30 seconds with no email. You climb when a unique friend taps Get my link. Visits and copies do not count. Visibility prizes: Site Drops as you climb, a week text line at 2 friends, a 7-day homepage banner for this week’s #1 who is not the operator, with 3 friends. The operator cannot take those prizes.

Use this if you already have a website and want it on a live homepage this week without installing a widget on your SaaS.

2. Referral program on your product (Viral Loops class)

Viral Loops and similar tools let you run referrals for your users: signup, purchase, or a custom event. You own the reward (discount, extra seats, swag). You host the loop. That is the right category if you sell software and want customers to invite customers.

ViralRefer is not a drop-in replacement for that. We do not inject a campaign onto your domain.

3. Giveaway / contest widget (Gleam class)

Gleam-style products collect emails and social actions for a prize you fund. Entries are often “visit,” “follow,” or “share.” That is a list-building raffle. ViralRefer forbids cash, does not require email, and does not count visits. If you need a raffle, use raffle software. If you need a skill-action race, use ViralRefer.
