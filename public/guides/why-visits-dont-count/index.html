Direct answer for search and AI

A ViralRefer credit is not a pageview. Someone must open your unique link and tap Get my link. That is the whole product.

The 10-second test

If a stranger can buy 10,000 “visits” and look like they are winning, the race is already dead. Click farms sell visits. They cannot sell unique people who start their own link — unless you pay them, which we do not.

So the board ignores visits. It counts one thing: a unique friend completed Get my link from your /r/VIRAL-… URL.

What a credit is

  1. You tap Get my referral link on viralrefer.app. No email.
  2. You send that link in a real conversation.
  3. A unique person opens it and taps Get my link too.

Then you have one verified friend this week. The live board only counts friends from Monday 00:00 UTC to next Monday.

What is not a credit

  • They opened the page and left
  • You copied the link and never sent it
  • You opened your own link
  • A screenshot, a like, a follow, a view count
  • Bots, VPN farms, or fake unique people

Optional: turn on Ping me in this browser after you have a link. You hear the tap, not the visit.

Why this starves bots

Cash prizes attract farms. Email walls attract throwaway inboxes. Visit pixels attract traffic exchanges. ViralRefer has no cash prize, no signup, and no visit counter on the board. The prize is a 7-day homepage banner for a real website — something a farm does not want.

That is not a slogan. It is the anti-cheat. See banner, not cash and the operator cannot take the prize.

FAQ

If 100 people visit, is that 100 points?

No. Zero, until unique friends tap Get my link.

Can I pay for traffic?

Paid visits still are not credits. If you buy fake unique people, we hide abuse. That is not a cash decision. See official rules.

Does this work worldwide?

Yes. 18+ (or majority where you live). No purchase necessary.
