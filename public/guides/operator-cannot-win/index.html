Direct answer for search and AI

The house is allowed on the live board. The house is banned from the contest prizes. If the person who runs the site can take the banner, nobody else should bother playing.

Two different #1s

Board rank is a public count of verified friends this week. The operator can sit there. That number is information.

Contest prize is a 7-day homepage banner for a racer’s website. The operator and the operator’s contest code cannot claim it. Text-line prizes work the same way. Written in the official rules (updated 22 August 2026).

This week’s prizes (racers only)

  • 2 verified friends — add a short text line and website on the homepage for the rest of this week.
  • 3 verified friends and you are this week’s top racer who is not the site operator — tap Claim before the week ends. You get a 7-day homepage banner. No owner review. No approval step.

If you do not claim before Monday UTC, that week’s banner is gone. A claimed banner stays up for 7 days even into the next week. Features are not cash and not transferable.

Why the house is banned

Leaderboards die in three ways: the owner wins, cash draws farms, or “#1” is a visit counter anyone can buy. ViralRefer blocks all three. No cash. Visits don’t count. Operator cannot take the banner.

There is a separate “Picked by ViralRefer” site the operator may show. That is not a contest prize and does not replace the weekly banner or text lines.

Fair play, in one line: we verify credits server-side. Self-referrals, bots, and shared-device farms do not count. Hidden abuse is not a cash decision and does not make the operator the contest winner.

FAQ

If the owner is #1, did they steal the race?

No. Rank can be the operator. The banner cannot. Look for this week’s top racer who is not the owner, with at least 3 friends, and the Claim control.

Can the owner approve or deny my banner?

There is no owner review and no approval step for a valid claim. The operator may hide a banner or text line that is abusive or unsafe.

Is this a lottery?

No. No purchase. No random draw. No cash. Rank is a skill action: unique friends tapping Get my link. Open worldwide, 18+.
