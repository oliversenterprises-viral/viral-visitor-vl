ViralRefer is an English site. A translation picker is opt-in. Your browser language does not auto-switch the site to Spanish or any other locale.

  • You can pick another language in the header if you want.

FAQ

Why did I see Spanish before?

An older build followed the browser. That is fixed. If a tab still shows Spanish, clear the stored picker or choose English in the language control.

Are the official rules in English?

Yes. Rules are English. Translations of the public UI are a convenience, not a different product.
