Traffic exchanges do not count

Direct answer for search and AI

Surf bars, hit exchanges, and auto-surf send visits. ViralRefer ignores visits. A credit is a unique friend who opens your link and taps Get my link. There is no cash prize, so farms have nothing to harvest.

Why exchanges fail here

Those networks pay people to sit on a page for seconds. That is a hit. Hits are not the skill action. Skill action, not a hit · Why visits don’t count.

What to do instead

Send one honest sentence to people who already talk to you. Copying is not a send. Share without spam.

FAQ

If 1,000 exchange users land, is that 1,000 friends?

Zero, until unique people tap Get my link from your link.

No. It is a free worldwide referral race. Prize is homepage visibility, not hits, not cash.
