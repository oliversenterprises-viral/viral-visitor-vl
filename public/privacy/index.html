Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

ViralRefer is a free worldwide referral leaderboard. You can get a link and climb the board without creating an account or giving an email address.

What we collect

  • Referral codes and share events needed to rank the public board
  • Network address and browser user-agent, used to block self-referrals, bots, and abuse
  • Optional website URL if you claim the homepage banner feature
  • Anonymous funnel events (landing, get-link, share) for site health

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not require a bank, wallet, or login for public use. There is no cash prize and we do not collect payment details.

How long we keep it

Referral and claim records stay as long as the leaderboard runs so ranks stay honest. Owner tools may delete test or abuse rows.
