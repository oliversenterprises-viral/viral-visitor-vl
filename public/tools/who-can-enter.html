Who can enter

ViralRefer is a skill-based referral race, not a lottery and not a cash contest.

You can enter if

  • You are a natural person (not a bot or a company account racing for the banner)
  • You are 18+, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher
  • Local law does not prohibit this kind of promotional contest

You cannot take contest prizes if

  • You operate ViralRefer, or you are an officer, agent, or household member of the operator
  • You try to credit yourself (self-taps, extra browsers, the same human twice)
  • You buy hits, surf bars, or fake friends

No purchase is necessary. The prize is homepage visibility (Site Drops and a 7-day banner), not cash. Full text lives on the rules page.

Lawton, Oklahoma is the operator’s city. The race itself is worldwide.
