# Content Quality Rubric — ViralRefer Traffic Booster

Score each piece **Pass / Revise / Reject** before publish.

## Must have (all Pass)

1. **Value** — Teaches something actionable about referral sharing or the platform
2. **Accuracy** — Claims match live rules, prizes, and mechanics
3. **CTA** — Clear path to "Get your free ViralRefer link in 30 seconds" with UTM
4. **Tone** — Exciting, transparent, empowering; no hype or guaranteed earnings
5. **Compliance** — US 18+, no purchase necessary, rules link where prizes mentioned
6. **Shareability** — Hook in first line; optional copy-paste share template

## Should have

- Primary keyword used naturally (not stuffed)
- Social proof (leaderboard, participant count, anonymized story)
- Platform-specific detail (no-signup, live board, 2026 campaign)
- Repurpose note: how this becomes 3+ social posts

## Reject if

- Spammy, misleading, or black-hat tactics suggested
- Guaranteed income or "#1 overnight" promises
- Fake engagement, bot traffic, purchased links
- Violates target platform community rules
- No genuine value beyond promoting the link
